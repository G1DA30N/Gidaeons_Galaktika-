# Privacy Policy Draft

Gidaeon's Galaktika does not collect, sell, transmit, or share personal data with the developer.

The extension runs locally in Firefox and stores only local preferences needed for its interface and behavior. When the optional local helper is installed, it runs on the user's computer and communicates only through Firefox native messaging. It does not open a localhost network listener.

The helper may process video or audio URLs from supported sites to generate waveforms or download media at the user's request. Processing occurs locally on the user's machine using locally installed tools such as `yt-dlp` and `ffmpeg`. Downloaded files are saved only to a user-selected local folder.

No analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers, or developer-operated servers are used by the extension.
